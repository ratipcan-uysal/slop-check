# Source-based edits

Use for summaries, translations, practical guides, and revisions of supplied prose. This reference makes the skill's fidelity rule concrete; it does not require a new inventory for every ordinary edit.

## Review the change, not just the new sentence

Compare the source and revised passage for actor, action, sequence, condition, exception, and certainty. Preserve whatever the source actually supplies. Do not fill a missing actor or duration with a plausible invention. If an instruction becomes ambiguous, return to the source rather than guessing.

Natural language does not require staccato fragments. Keep linked thoughts in a complete sentence when their relationship matters. Fix broken inflections and punctuation before reporting a style improvement. A vivid replacement that asserts more than the source is a failed edit.

| Source wording | Preserve |
|---|---|
| “Ödül sistemine çevirmek gerekmez.” | Optionality; this does not mean “Ödül sistemine çevirme.” |
| “Bunu yapabileceğini anlat.” | Possibility; this does not mean “Bunu yapacağını anlat.” |
| “Sakin, uyanık ve dinlemeye açık.” | Three distinct conditions, if the source requires them. |
| A particular family's two-week trial | An example, not a deadline for every reader. |

## Know what the detector matched

The Turkish three-item pattern counts word shapes, including nouns and conditions. It cannot determine that the third item is ornamental. The passive pattern can match `olabilir`, `gerekebilir`, and `gösterebilir` because they end in `ilir`. Those matches do not establish passive voice, and removing them may increase certainty.

Look at actual passages and retain necessary language. Record a legitimate match separately from an unresolved prose defect. Keep the raw metrics and the skill verdict; do not suppress flags or recalibrate thresholds to make one document pass.

## Tie results to versions

Save a baseline before revision. After meaning and grammar review, measure the revised text and record its file hashes. If the scope gained a table, index, or recovered source meaning, disclose that the numbers compare different content as well as different wording. Do not attribute every numerical change to style editing.

A real error discovered afterward must be corrected. Measure that changed version or label the older measurement accurately. Repeated edits solely to clear flags are forbidden; new content or a real defect is a reason to refresh evidence.
